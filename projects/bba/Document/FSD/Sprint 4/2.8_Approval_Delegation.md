## 2. Purpose and background

A Centre Director (or HQ Admin) can hand one of their own approval permissions — such as approving leave, voiding an attendance record or reviewing class photos — to a staff member, with a reason and an optional end date, and can take it back at any time. This keeps approvals moving when the Centre Director is away. The staff member sees the matching requests in My approvals.

## 4. Users, roles and access

| User | Scope | Rights / Responsibilities |
|---|---|---|
| HQ Admin (BBA IT) | Every country, brand and centre. | Full rights; manages approval routes and settings; can give extra permissions to staff of any centre; can decide an approval step on behalf of the role that owns it. |
| Centre Director | Own centre. | Gives extra permissions to staff of the centre; changes centre-level settings they are allowed to change; approves the centre's requests. |
| Senior Teacher / staff member given a permission | Class / centre. | Uses the permission given (for example approving leave, voiding attendance, reviewing photos) until it is taken back or ends. |
| System | Whole system. | Sends requests to approvers by route, works out impact, ends permissions on their end date, keeps history, sends notifications. |

**General rules:** only users with Administration rights can open the Administration screens; actions a user may not take are not shown; history can only be added to, never edited or deleted; standard lists offer search, filters, sorting, 20 / 50 / 100 rows per page and export of the filtered list.

## 6. Requirements by user story

### 6.1 BBA-50: US-001 - Approval delegation

#### User Story

As a Centre Director, I want to hand an approval permission I hold to a staff member of my centre for a period of time, so that approvals do not stop while I am away.

#### Functional Requirements

| FR ID | Requirement |
|---|---|
| FR-001 | A Centre Director gives a staff member of their own centre a permission the Centre Director holds (for example approving leave, voiding attendance, reviewing photos). A reason is required, the end date is optional, and the "I confirm this person should hold it" box must be ticked. HQ Admin can give permissions to staff of any centre. |
| FR-002 | The list of permissions given shows Person, Permission, Granted by, From, Until ("No end date" when empty) and Status (Active / Expired / Revoked, with the date taken back); search by name; filter by permission, centre and status; export. |
| FR-003 | Taking a permission back takes effect at once; a permission ends by itself on its end date. |
| FR-004 | The staff member gets a notification in the app when a permission is given, taken back or ends. |
| FR-005 | The staff member can use the permission as soon as it is given; what a user can do is the sum of their roles, extra permissions and scope. For an approval permission, the matching requests appear in the staff member's My approvals. |
| FR-006 | History records every time a permission is given, taken back or ends. |
| FR-007 | Permissions that can be given: Approve leave requests, Review class update photos, Void or delete attendance records, Assign teachers to a class, Move a class's children up, Manage users of their own centre. The first three are approval permissions and match the routes labelled "can be delegated" (Child leave, Photos, Attendance void). |
| FR-008 | Revoke opens a confirmation window "Revoke <permission>?" with the text "<Person> loses it now and is told in the app."; choosing Revoke takes the permission back at once. |

#### Functional Flow - Main

1. The Centre Director opens Extra permissions and chooses Grant permission (FR-001).
2. The Centre Director picks a staff member of the centre and a permission they hold (FR-007), enters the reason and end date, and ticks the confirmation box (FR-001; VAL-001, VAL-002, VAL-003).
3. The system records the permission as Active and sends a notification (FR-003, FR-004).
4. The staff member uses the permission, for example approving a leave request in My approvals (FR-005; AS-DLG-02).

#### Functional Flow - Alternative

1. The Centre Director chooses Revoke and confirms: the permission becomes Revoked at once and the staff member is notified (FR-003, FR-004, FR-008).
2. HQ Admin gives a permission to a staff member of any centre (FR-001).
3. On the end date: the permission becomes Expired and the staff member is notified (FR-003, FR-004).

#### Functional Flow - Exception

1. A permission the Centre Director does not hold: not offered in the list (VAL-001).
2. Missing reason: not saved (VAL-002).
3. Confirmation box not ticked: not given (VAL-003).

#### Business Rules

| BR ID | Rule |
|---|---|
| BR-001 | A Centre Director can only give permissions to staff of their own centre, and only permissions they hold themselves; a reason is required. |
| BR-002 | An extra permission ends when it is taken back or on its end date; the staff member is told in both cases. |

#### Validation Rules

| VAL ID | Condition | System response |
|---|---|---|
| VAL-001 | A permission the Centre Director does not hold, or a staff member from another centre. | Not offered in the selection list. |
| VAL-002 | Missing reason, or an end date before today. | Not saved; shows "Enter a reason for granting this permission." at the reason field. |
| VAL-003 | "I confirm this person should hold it" not ticked. | Grant does nothing; the confirmation box is marked as required. |

## 8. Appendix
