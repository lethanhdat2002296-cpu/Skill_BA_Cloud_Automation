## 2. Purpose and background

A Centre Director (or HQ Admin) hands one of their own approval permissions — approving leave, voiding attendance, reviewing photos… — to a staff member, with a reason and an end date, and can take it back, so that approvals do not stop while the approver is away. The staff member sees the matching requests in My approvals.

## 6. Functional requirements by user story

### 6.1 BBA-50: US-001 - Approval delegation

#### User Story

As a Centre Director, I want to hand an approval permission I hold to a staff member of my centre for a period of time, so that approvals do not stop while I am away.

#### Functional Requirements

| FR ID | Requirement |
|---|---|
| FR-001 | A Centre Director gives a staff member of their own centre a permission the Centre Director holds (for example approving leave, voiding attendance, reviewing photos). A reason and a From – Until period are required: From defaults to the current date, can be changed and cannot be before today; Until is required and cannot be before From; the "I confirm this person should hold it" box must be ticked. HQ Admin can give permissions to staff of any centre. |
| FR-002 | The list of permissions given shows Person, Permission, Granted by, From, Until and Status (Scheduled / Active / Expired / Revoked, with the date taken back); search by name; filter by permission, centre and status; export. |
| FR-003 | Taking a permission back takes effect at once and applies to Active or Scheduled permissions; a permission stays valid to the end of its Until date and becomes Expired automatically from the next day. |
| FR-004 | The staff member gets an in-app notification when a permission is given, when a Scheduled permission starts, when it is taken back and when it ends. |
| FR-005 | The staff member can use the permission from the From date to the end of the Until date; what a user can do is the sum of their roles, extra permissions and scope. For an approval permission, the matching requests appear in the staff member's My approvals during that period. |
| FR-006 | History records every time a permission is given, taken back or ends. |
| FR-007 | **FR-007:** Permissions that can be given fall into 2 groups. **(a) Approval permissions:** Approve leave requests, Review class update photos, Void or delete attendance records. An approval permission appears in the Permission field only when HQ Admin allows that request type to be delegated ("Can be delegated" ticked in Administration › Approval routes). Example: if the Attendance void route does not have "Can be delegated" ticked, "Void or delete attendance records" is not in the list, and only the Centre Director can approve voiding attendance. Once given, the matching requests appear in the holder's My approvals. **(b) Action permissions:** Assign teachers to a class, Move a class's children up, Manage users of their own centre. Once given, the holder can open the matching function within their centre. The holder cannot pass the permission on to anyone else. |
| FR-008 | Revoke opens a confirmation window "Revoke <permission>?" with the text "<Person> loses it now and is told in the app."; choosing Revoke takes the permission back at once. |
| FR-009 | A permission whose From date is after today is recorded as Scheduled; the holder cannot use it yet. On the From date, the system makes it Active and sends a notification (FR-004). A Scheduled permission can be taken back in the same way as an Active one (FR-008). |

#### Functional Flow - Main

1. The Centre Director opens Extra permissions and chooses Grant permission (FR-001).
2. The Centre Director picks a staff member of the centre and a permission they hold (FR-007), enters the reason, keeps or changes the start date (From, today by default), picks the end date (Until), and ticks the confirmation box (FR-001; VAL-001, VAL-002, VAL-003, VAL-004).
3. The system records the permission as Active if From is today, or Scheduled if From is after today, and sends a notification (FR-003, FR-004, FR-009).
4. From the From date to the end of the Until date, the staff member uses the permission, for example approving a leave request in My approvals (FR-005; AS-DLG-02).

#### Functional Flow - Alternative

1. The Centre Director chooses Revoke and confirms: the permission becomes Revoked at once and the staff member is notified (FR-003, FR-004, FR-008).
2. HQ Admin gives a permission to a staff member of any centre (FR-001).
3. After the Until date: the permission becomes Expired and the staff member is notified (FR-003, FR-004).
4. On the From date of a Scheduled permission: the permission becomes Active and the staff member is notified (FR-009, FR-004).

#### Functional Flow - Exception

1. A permission the Centre Director does not hold: not offered in the list (VAL-001).
2. Missing reason: not saved (VAL-002).
3. Confirmation box not ticked: not given (VAL-003).
4. Until missing, Until before From, or From before today: not saved (VAL-004).

#### Business Rules

| BR ID | Rule |
|---|---|
| BR-001 | A Centre Director can only give permissions to staff of their own centre, and only permissions they hold themselves; a reason is required. |
| BR-002 | An extra permission is valid only from the From date to the end of the Until date; it ends when it is taken back or once the Until date has passed; the staff member is told in both cases. |
| BR-003 | Every extra permission has a set period: From cannot be before the day it is given, Until is required and cannot be before From; no permission is given without an end date. |

#### Validation Rules

| VAL ID | Condition | System response |
|---|---|---|
| VAL-001 | A permission the Centre Director does not hold, or a staff member from another centre. | Not offered in the selection list. |
| VAL-002 | Missing reason. | Not saved; shows "Enter a reason for granting this permission." at the reason field. |
| VAL-003 | "I confirm this person should hold it" not ticked. | Grant does nothing; the confirmation box is marked as required (Prototype). |
| VAL-004 | Until missing; Until before From; or From before today. | Not saved; shows at the field concerned: "Choose an end date." (Until missing); "The end date cannot be before the start date." (Until before From); "The start date cannot be before today." (From before today). |

## 8. Appendix

### Appendix A. Glossary

| Term | Meaning in this FRD |
|---|---|
| Extra permission | A permission a Centre Director (or HQ Admin) gives a staff member of the centre on top of their role, possibly with an end date. |
| Can be delegated | Label on an approval route whose approval permission can be handed to a staff member through Extra permissions. |
| My approvals | The screen holding every request waiting for the user's decision (Waiting tab) and those already decided (Done tab); called Approvals inbox before version 0.3. |
| Scheduled | Status of a permission that has been given but whose From date has not come yet; the holder cannot use it yet. On the From date it becomes Active automatically. |
